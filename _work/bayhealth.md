## What I did
- Audited the site to find what would break in a move, then oversaw the migration from a large web design firm's hosting to a smaller provider with no significant downtime.
- Rebuilt the whole site in Divi, replacing the previous custom-field-based layout. It looks the same as before but is much simpler underneath.
- The point of the rebuild: the owners can now edit pages themselves without calling a developer.
