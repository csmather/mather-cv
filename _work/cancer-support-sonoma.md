## My role & impact
- Led a full redesign of the nonprofit's Wix site: restructured the navigation around how clients actually use the org, rebuilt the homepage and services pages, and standardized typography and brand colors sitewide.
- Rebuilt the services section around tap-to-expand card galleries, deliberately avoiding hover-dependent interactions that fail on mobile.
- Staged the entire redesign as an unpublished draft alongside the live site, shared it with staff through a test-site link for review, and cut over cleanly at launch.
- Continue as volunteer webmaster: maintaining the program calendar, provider profiles, and event promotion in direct coordination with staff.
